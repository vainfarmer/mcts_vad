## 1. Mechanism and motivation (closures respected)

FDS = **4fps (24-frame) directed-scan sampling on hard-category videos — frame DENSITY at fixed window+placement**, the untested axis after KFD (placement, v173 REVERT-closed) and FSD (granularity, v182 REVERT-closed). Flag ON and pipeline-resolved `videomae_category` ∈ {Shooting, RoadAccidents, Shoplifting, Explosion} (`pipeline.FDS_CATEGORIES`): Stage-4 DIRECTED scan (both `scan_segment` paths: `_directed_llm_scan` and `discovery.discover` fallback) sends each **already-selected** 6s segment to the VLM with frames doubled ~12 (2fps) -> ~24 (4fps) — same 6s window, same uniform placement, density doubled. Implementation: re-decode at 2x `preprocess.fps` to get a dense frame pool (all other preprocess params unchanged), `_sample_keyframes` re-samples by time range from the dense pool; `scan_segment` call/prompt/JSON schema untouched. Candidate build (CLIP-loc directed + generic prefilter + min_pass/max_scan caps) is computed on the original 2fps grid (selection byte-identical to flag-off); merge/NMS/classify/BUE/CBT all untouched; `video_data['segments']` not mutated; FCCS comparative path unaffected (FDS arms only when not `_fccs`).

Respected closures:
- **KFD placement closed (v173)**: FDS does NOT move frame placement within the window — same uniform time-grid, only denser pool.
- **FSD granularity closed (v182)**: FDS does NOT split the 6s segment grid — segment windows are byte-identical.
- **Candidate-relocation family closed (CGR/CCR v164–v169)**: FDS touches no candidate lists, counts, confidences, or boundaries.
- **CNV veto family closed on harm (v171/v172)**: FDS never zeroes confidence and never removes a detection.
- **TCP prompt-side closed (v177)**: FDS changes frames, not prompt text — scan prompt byte-identical.
- v192–v195 train-localization/research no-gos untouched: FDS needs no train labels.

## 10. Family update — what is now refuted / validated

- **Refuted (measured, valid cross-run null)**: frame-DENSITY doubling (2fps -> 4fps, 12 -> 24 frames) at fixed window+placement on the directed scan is NOT a trustworthy mAP lever on the hard-88. The point gain +0.01968 is exactly at the +/-0.019 cross-run VLM noise band, per-video mean delta is negative, P(delta>0)=0.473 = coin flip, and 3/4 hard categories regressed. With KFD (placement, v173) and FSD (granularity, v182) already closed, **all three frame-sampling axes of the directed scan are now measured-closed**: placement (uniform vs first-k), granularity (6s vs 3s), density (2fps vs 4fps). The directed scan's frame budget is at a measured plateau — the VLM does not convert MORE frames of the SAME window into better temporal detections (mirrors the v177 prompt-side ceiling: the recognition residual is not input-quantity-addressable).
- **Validated (process/instrument)**: zero-cost-input-changes verified exactly — VLM call totals equal across arms (1542 vs 1543, retry-level) with frame-tokens 1.88x, i.e. cost accounting cleanly separates call-count from token-count; `[FDS-VID]`/`[FDS-FIRE]` marker discipline (88 unconditional / 79 fires) held on the first try; FCCS confound grep-gate held in both arms (0/0).
- **Validated (design choice)**: the committed 2fps/~12-frame directed scan stands confirmed — doubling density measurably failed to help, so the committed budget is not under-provisioned on this axis.
- **Still open**: the hard-category recognition residual (Shoplifting cand-miss lineage) — now closed on frames-placement, frames-granularity, frames-density, and scan-prompt axes; any future attempt must change WHAT the VLM is asked to do with the window (a new mechanism family), not how many/how the frames are presented.
- **Caveat on record**: cross-run mode carries ~+/-0.019 unpaired VLM variance on top of the bootstrap CI; this A/B proves ABSENCE of the required positive evidence (P=0.473 << 0.9), not presence of harm. A paired design does not exist for input-changing mechanisms; re-arming FDS would require one.
