## 1. THE MECHANISM, and WHY the objective is the FA rate, not mAP

The pipeline makes only SEGMENT-level decisions; nothing asks "does this video, as a whole,
contain an event that breaks its own baseline?" VLM absolute confidence on isolated clips is
uncalibrated (v143 proved scan_segment pins ~0.9 on isolated clips), so Normal videos carry
hallucinated detections that nothing downstream can remove.

The v9 oracle fixes the objective: **deleting EVERY Normal-video FP is worth only +0.00253 mAP**
(analysis_v9_normal_fp.py). mAP therefore CANNOT be the objective of a Normal-video cleanup
mechanism — a perfect version of it is invisible to mAP. The objective is the **Normal-video
FALSE-ALARM RATE**: fraction of the 150 Normal-category test videos carrying >= 1 surviving
(conf > 0) detection, arm-ON vs arm-OFF, plus mean surviving FP detections and FP-seconds per
Normal video. mAP enters only as a non-inferiority constraint (paired delta >= -0.003,
abs >= 0.16454), plus a harm check (vetoed detections overlapping GT at IoU>=0.1 must be ~0).
CNV was judged against exactly that objective — not re-judged as an mAP mechanism, and the FA
enthusiasm was not allowed to override the mAP/GT harm signal. Both failed anyway.

CNV is a ONE-SIDED CONFIDENCE VETO at the TERMINAL slot (after BUE/CBT, on the genuinely-final
list). It NEVER moves a window, NEVER adds a detection, NEVER re-ranks. Veto = confidence := 0.0,
detection KEPT in the list (count-frozen, index-aligned — FM2 assert len unchanged).

- STAGE 1 (video-level global-null gate): veto ALL detections of a video iff ALL THREE
  independent cached signal families are negative: (a) MIL video-level evidence
  top-k mean(_mil_scores) < tau_mil=0.20; (b) fused CLIP category curve in-video outlierness
  max((x-median)/MAD) < tau_z=1.5; (c) motion-energy outlier fraction (frames above
  median+3*MAD) < tau_mot=0.01. v172 TIGHTEN adds a NECESSARY fourth conjunct: the video's own
  stored videomae_category must be 'Normal'.
- STAGE 2 (segment-level in-video surprisal veto): on videos PASSING stage 1, veto any detection
  whose window-aggregated curve mass is not an in-video outlier (v171: z_window < 0.5; v172
  TIGHTEN: z_window < 0, a strict subset) AND the window does not contain the video's global
  curve argmax.

Thresholds are PRINCIPLED STRUCTURAL DEFAULTS — verified (not fitted) on TRAIN-split videos
only (section 4). Zero new VLM/CLIP/MIL calls: every input is a cached signal.


two sessions' REF arms differ — 0.17266 in the v171 run vs 0.16919 in the v172 run: each run

The evidence for HARM rather than under-fire:
1. Both variants vetoed true detections at 6-13x the allowed budget (gt_vetoed 13 and 6 vs

   harm was present at both ends.
3. The stage-1 whole-video gate — the only part that could produce a 25% FA-rate reduction —
   is structurally near-dead by train-verification (0/3 normals fire the conjunct), and the
   detection-level veto that does all the work has a structural ceiling on the video-level
   metric (v172: 7 Normal-video vetoes, 0 videos became detection-free).
4. The signals themselves cannot separate the classes at the veto points: the pipeline's true
   detections and its hallucinated Normal-video detections both sit on in-video outliers of
   the same cached curves, so in-video surprisal is not a normality signal.

CONSEQUENCE / what a future session may and may not do: do NOT re-tune CNV's thresholds and do
NOT re-propose a confidence veto built on the MIL-topk / CLIP-curve-z / motion-fraction triple —
