## 1. Mechanism and motivation (closures respected)

SVA = **zero-cost cross-video confidence-LEVEL calibration from the directed scan's OWN per-segment verdict aggregate** — the third signal variant of the +0.0518 cross-video level oracle (v11 diagnostic), after the MIL variant (v183, CLOSED at Phase-0: hard-88 -0.01253) and the VLM-read variant (v196 VLC, VOID M4). A video whose top scan segments ALL read high is more likely truly anomalous than a video with one isolated high segment. level = mean of the top-3 retained scan-segment verdict confidences (clamped [0,1]; mean of ALL when <3 segments; None/NA when none retained), then every FINAL detection: `conf_new = conf_old * (0.5 + 0.5*level)`. Unlike v196 (a dedicated extra video-level VLM read costing +1 detect call per ndet>=1 video), SVA reuses an ALREADY-EXISTING signal — zero new VLM/CLIP/MIL calls by construction.

Respected closures:
- **v183 CLC (MIL level signal)**: SVA carries NO MIL anywhere — the level signal is the scan VLM's own verdict aggregate, not model_mil top-3 snippet means.
- **Count/window/order-frozen discipline (v167 CGR-M contract)**: only `a['confidence']` is written; detections are kept, index-aligned, t_start/t_end/anomaly_type/order bit-frozen; FM2 assert on count in `_sva_calibrate`.
- **CNV/SNH veto-family harm closures (v171/v172/v179)**: SVA never zeroes confidence and never removes a detection (multiplier in [0.5,1.0]).
- **Terminal-slot ordering (v196/v197 precedent)**: fires AFTER CNV/SNH/VLC/DSV on the genuinely-final final_anomalies, immediately before DetectionResult is built — conf-only, so the effect is mAP-only with clean attribution.
- **v199 FDS closure**: SVA does not touch frames/density/placement — it reads verdicts the committed 2fps scan already produced.
- v192–v195 train-localization/research no-gos untouched: SVA needs no train labels.
